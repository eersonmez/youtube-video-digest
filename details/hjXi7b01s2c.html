OpenAI’s ChatGPT sales assistant supports account planning and meeting preparation.

OpenAI · 2026-07-28T14:20:18+00:00 · ChatGPT Work for Sales: Account Planning and Meeting Prep

What This Video Is About

This short OpenAI demonstration shows how a personal sales assistant can help sellers turn account context and sales priorities into a concrete plan for the day and a prepared customer meeting. The stated business problem is consistent execution across a sales organization, particularly when leadership introduces new sales plays. The assistant is presented as a way to help each seller operate with the guidance and context associated with a top seller.

The workflow begins with a seller asking the assistant to “plan my day.” The assistant draws on Slack, Google Calendar, Salesforce, and other business context to identify the seller’s highest-priority actions. It goes beyond producing an agenda: it recognizes that a catalyst workshop is scheduled for the following day. The workshop is described as a new sales play intended to build customer interest and generate pipeline.

The assistant then supports the substantial preparation required for that workshop. It creates a command center containing opportunity context, stakeholder mapping, and workshop objectives; generates a PowerPoint and customer-specific mobile mockups; adds a narrated, animated preview video; and posts the package to the customer’s Slack channel. The video’s central claim is that this kind of assistance can extend a seller’s capacity and embed new sales plays into the company’s normal operating rhythm.

  • Ask the sales assistant to plan the seller’s day.
