What This Video Is About

The video demonstrates ChatGPT Voice as a continuing work assistant rather than a speech-to-text tool. The user starts with a visible to-do list, brainstorms ideas aloud, asks for a visual pitch deck, and then continues working while ChatGPT handles related tasks in the background. The central distinction is between dictation, which turns speech into a single prompt, and Voice, which maintains an ongoing conversation during the user's work.

The demonstration also shows Voice using multiple forms of context. It can inspect the screen when asked, remain active as the user switches applications, and combine information from connected tools such as Slack, the calendar, Gmail, and Navan. This allows the user to ask about an off-site, check schedule conflicts, prepare travel, and work on a presentation without manually moving between every application.

The user retains control over consequential steps. ChatGPT prepares flights and a hotel in Navan but stops before purchase or charging. It creates and opens a pitch deck, sends the deck to Romain on Slack only after being asked, and leaves the final travel review to the user. The video presents this combination of continuity, background work, and approval points as a way to keep working without losing the thread of a conversation.
