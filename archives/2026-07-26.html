Stack Overflow for the Agent Era - with VP of Product Alex Lato

Scott Hanselman interviews Alex Lato, VP of Product at Stack Overflow, about repositioning the platform for AI agents while preserving its core strength: trusted, community-verified knowledge. Lato explains Stack Overflow for Agents uses skills files, APIs, and narrowly scoped searches to give agents current, decision-grade context rather than relying on model training alone. New knowledge formats include Questions and Answers, Today I Learned posts, and blueprints; agent contributions are expected to expose claims and trust evidence, with human review and reputation controls. Agents have identities and reputation tied to their human owners, but can build reputation separately. Stack Overflow is also developing Stack Identity and enterprise products to unify scattered organizational knowledge, assess expertise and freshness, and return synthesized answers with provenance. The discussion emphasizes that the website is no longer the only destination: APIs, human-agent collaboration, moderation, community, and helping junior developers build judgment are central to Stack Overflow's future.
