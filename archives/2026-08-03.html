How Much Water AI Data Centers Actually Use. And What Can Be Done About It. — With Christophe Beck

AI data center expansion poses significant risks to natural resources, with estimates suggesting that by 2030, AI will require the incremental power of India and the drinking water of the United States annually. Currently, many projects are delayed due to resource scarcity or community opposition. Traditional cooling methods using large AC units and cooling towers consume substantial water.
